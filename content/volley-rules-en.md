# Volley Mode Rules & Illustrated Guide

## 1. Goal of Volley Mode

![Volley arena with numbered bricks, cannon and danger line](assets/volley-guide/01_volley_arena.jpg)

- Launch a complete volley of balls from the cannon at the bottom of the arena.
- Every numbered brick shows its remaining **HP**. Each normal hit removes one HP unless an active effect changes the damage.
- Destroy every brick that counts as a stage objective to win. Permanent obstacles do not normally need to be destroyed.
- You lose if a live objective brick or indestructible obstacle crosses the **danger line** after a turn, unless a Rescue Ward saves the run.
- Volley does not use the moving paddle found in Classic mode.

## 2. Aim and Launch

![Aim upward and release to launch the volley](assets/volley-guide/02_aim_launch.jpg)

- Drag upward inside the arena to set the firing direction, then release to launch.
- Balls leave the cannon one after another along the selected path.
- Balls bounce from walls, the ceiling, bricks and solid angled rails. Bank shots help reach protected targets.
- Drag back into the cancel area if you want to cancel the shot.
- You cannot start a second shot until the current volley has finished or has been recalled.

## 3. Bricks, HP and Ricochets

![Numbered bricks and angled collision surfaces](assets/volley-guide/03_hp_and_bounces.jpg)

- The number on a brick is its remaining HP.
- Triangular bricks have real angled collision surfaces and deflect balls differently from rectangular bricks.
- Rails redirect balls. Some remain fixed while others descend with the formation.
- Repeated ricochets through a crowded path are usually stronger than a straight shot.
- Steel or indestructible bricks reflect balls and are normally obstacles, but they can still trigger a loss if they cross the danger line.

## 4. First Return and the Next Launch

![The first returning ball selects the next launch position](assets/volley-guide/04_first_return.jpg)

- The first ball that returns naturally to the floor records its horizontal position.
- After all balls return, that point normally becomes the next launch position.
- **Sticky Return** preserves the old launch point. **Magnetic Return** pulls the recorded point toward the center.
- Recall is not a natural return, so it should not be used to choose an exact new launch position.

## 5. Turns, Descent and the Danger Line

![The wall descends after a completed turn](assets/volley-guide/05_wall_descends.jpg)

- Every completed or recalled volley consumes one turn.
- After the volley resolves, surviving bricks, rails and live bonus nodes descend by one row.
- Regenerating bricks may recover HP, and moving bricks may change location between turns.
- New balls collected during a shot join future volleys after the current turn resolves.
- If a live target or indestructible blocker breaches the lower line, the stage fails unless a Rescue Ward pushes the formation back.

## 6. Controls

![Bag, Items, 3X Speed, Recall and Pause controls](assets/volley-guide/06_game_controls.jpg)

- **Bag:** view owned charms and prepare a compatible effect when permitted.
- **Items:** opens compatible items from the shared game inventory. These are Classic items adapted by the game for use between volleys, not Volley-exclusive charms.
- **3X Speed:** press and hold while balls are in flight; release to return to normal speed.
- **Recall:** ends the current volley early. It still consumes the turn and the wall still descends.
- **Pause / Settings:** pause the attempt, adjust options or resume the run.

## 7. Bonus Nodes and Pickups

![Extra-ball, coin and shield bonus nodes](assets/volley-guide/07_bonus_nodes.jpg)

- **+BALL:** permanently adds the displayed number of balls to later volleys in the current attempt.
- **Carrier bricks:** may release extra balls or a positive item when destroyed.
- **Coins:** collectible nodes can award coins.
- **Shield:** grants a Rescue Ward up to the allowed limit. A ward can prevent one danger-line breach.
- Other nodes can award score, random items, star energy or speed effects.

## 8. Special Bricks

![Special brick types used by Volley stages](assets/volley-guide/08_special_bricks.jpg)

- **Armored:** requires sustained hits; stronger variants have greater durability.
- **Regenerating:** restores HP between turns up to its original value.
- **Explosive:** damages nearby bricks when destroyed.
- **Speed / Slow pass-through:** allows passage while changing ball speed.
- **Black-hole portal:** teleports a ball between linked portal locations.
- **Key and Lock:** collect the key to unlock its linked targets.
- **Switch and Ghost Gate:** a switch changes the collision state of linked gates.
- **Chain and Moving bricks:** linked blocks can react together, while moving blocks reposition between turns.

## 9. Boss Battles

![Boss portrait and four guards protecting the boss core](assets/volley-guide/10_boss_core.jpg)

- The final playable stage of every world is a boss encounter.
- Four shield guards protect the central Boss Core.
- Destroy all four guards first, then attack the exposed core and clear the remaining required targets.
- Boss arenas add bank-shot rails and a world-specific mechanic.

## 11. Win, Loss and Useful Tips

![Stage-clear and danger-line-failure outcomes](assets/volley-guide/12_clear_or_fail.jpg)

- **Stage Clear:** destroy all required objective bricks, even if unbreakable obstacles remain.
- **Wall Breached:** a required or indestructible brick reaches the danger line without an available Rescue Ward.
- If a rewarded continue is available and the reward completes, the wall is pushed back four rows.
- Collect +BALL nodes early, watch the lowest live brick, and use side-wall angles to create long ricochets.
- Remember: **Recall costs a turn** and does not stop the wall from descending.
